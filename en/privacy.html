Privacy policy

Updated 16 October 2026

La France en clair prepares you for the naturalisation civic exam. It is independent of the French Ministry of the Interior and does not issue a certificate.

Publisher and contact

Cesar Mauricio Acuna Herrera publishes La France en clair. Use the support link below for questions about the app or your data.

App data

Your answers, progress, bookmarks, preferences and test-exam history are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to the publisher. The app has no account, advertising, advertising tracking or analytics service.

Purchases

On iOS, Apple handles the one-time purchase and restore through your Apple Account. The app verifies access through StoreKit. The publisher does not receive payment details or your answer history. Apple's privacy policy applies to Apple purchases.

Audio and notifications

French recordings are included in the app. The device's French voice can replace a missing recording. No text or study history is sent to an online speech service. Study reminders are optional local notifications.

Backups and deletion

Apple or Google may include study data in device backups, depending on your settings. The app has no sync service of its own. To erase app data on iOS, delete the app in system storage settings. On Android, clear the app's storage. Manage backups separately in system settings. Deleting study data does not revoke an Apple purchase.

Links, website and support

Links open external sites with their own policies. Your answers and progress are not attached to links. GitHub Pages hosts this static website and may process technical logs, including IP addresses. This website adds no analytics or tracking cookies. If you contact support on GitHub, GitHub processes the information you choose to post. Requests are public. Do not post sensitive data. If you email us, we receive your email address and message content to respond to your request.
