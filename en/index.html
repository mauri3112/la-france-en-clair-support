Help and support

Updated 16 October 2026

La France en clair prepares you for the naturalisation civic exam. It is independent of the French Ministry of the Interior and does not issue a certificate.

Free questions and full access

210 of 420 questions are free. A one-time purchase in the iOS app unlocks every question and test exams, with no subscription. The App Store shows the price before purchase.

Restore a purchase

Use the Apple Account that made the purchase. In the app, open Learn, Settings, full access, then Restore purchases. You need an internet connection. Apple handles refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Languages and audio

Choose the app language in Settings. Optional study translations help with questions and vocabulary. Test exams are in French. Recordings work offline, with the device's French voice available if a recording is missing.

Progress and reminders

Progress stays on your device. Backups depend on your system settings. The app does not sync between devices. Turn reminders off in Settings or the system notification settings.

Report a problem

Open a support request with your device model, system and app version, and steps to reproduce the problem. For a content issue, include the question text or identifier. GitHub requests are public. Do not post personal documents, passwords or purchase receipts.
